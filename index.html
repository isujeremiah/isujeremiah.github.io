02 Research

Research

I study when quantum measurements and channels produce data that uniquely determines an underlying quantum state, drawing on tools from quantum tomography.

01 — Quantum information theory

Informational completeness & phase retrieval

When does the data from a quantum measurement or quantum channel uniquely identify a quantum state? I investigate these questions through the mathematical framework of quantum tomography.
