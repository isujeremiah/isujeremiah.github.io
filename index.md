# About Me

I am a PhD student in Mathematics at the University of Oklahoma, where I study the mathematical foundations of quantum information. My current work examines how quantum states can be reconstructed through phase retrieval techniques involving quantum measurements and quantum channels. I am also interested in quantum computing and the ways it can be applied to scientific and technological problems.
